# Acceptance Checklist

- WA inquiry can enter Inbox and be promoted to a project.
- Survey records measurements and evidence.
- RAB computes material/labor/machine/logistics/waste/markup.
- Quote approval establishes contract value.
- DP is auditable and production gate is enforced.
- Procurement tracks material demand.
- Workshop steps produce measurable progress.
- QC requires all mandatory checks to pass.
- Installation and accepted handover gate closure.
- Change orders alter commercial baseline explicitly.
- Risks, issues, decisions, approvals and evidence are queryable.
- Dashboard surfaces blockers, approvals, receivables and health.
- Outbound WA uses durable outbox and idempotency.
